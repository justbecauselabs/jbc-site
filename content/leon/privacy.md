# LEON – PRIVACY POLICY

_Last updated: 16 January 2026_

Welcome to Leon, an AI-powered meme generation application offered by JUST BECAUSE LABS LLC—an Ohio limited-liability company ("JBL," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our mobile applications and related services (collectively, the "Services"). This Policy follows best-practice guidance from prominent AI products and recent U.S. and international privacy-law developments.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

* **Account Data** – When you create an account via Apple Sign-In, we receive your Apple User ID and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address.
* **Meme Prompts & Input** – The text descriptions, ideas, and prompts you provide when generating memes.
* **Meme Content** – The captions, template selections, and humor persona choices you make.
* **Search Queries** – Your meme idea descriptions used to find appropriate templates.
* **Support & Feedback** – Messages, screenshots, and diagnostics you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

### Information Collected Automatically

* **Usage Data** – App interactions, features used, meme generation count, timestamp data, and session information.
* **Device Data** – Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and app version.
* **Template Usage Analytics** – Aggregated statistics on template popularity and usage trends.
* **Network Information** – IP address, general location derived from IP (country/region level only).

### Information from Third Parties

* **Apple Sign-In** – Authentication tokens and user identifiers provided by Apple.
* **Payment Processors** – Subscription status and purchase identifiers from RevenueCat/Apple (we do not receive or store payment card numbers).
* **AI Service Providers** – We send your prompts to AI providers to generate meme content; they may process this data according to their own policies.

### Information We Do NOT Collect

* **Photos or Camera Access** – Leon does not access your device camera or photo library for collection purposes. When you save memes to your device, we request temporary permission to write files only.
* **Location Data** – We do not collect precise GPS location data.
* **Contacts** – We do not access your contacts or address book.
* **Biometric Data** – We do not collect fingerprints, face scans, or other biometric information.

**No obligation to provide data:** If you decline to share certain information, some features may not function. For example, you cannot use the Services without creating an account.

7. **Bug Fixes & Debugging** – Identify and resolve technical issues.

### Safety & Compliance
8. **Content Moderation** – Monitor for policy violations including harassment, illegal content, and prompt-injection attacks.
9. **Fraud Prevention** – Detect and prevent abuse, unauthorized access, and fraudulent activity.
10. **Legal Compliance** – Satisfy law-enforcement requests, court orders, or regulatory requirements; enforce our Terms of Service; protect rights, safety, or property.

### Communications
11. **Service Announcements** – Notify you of important updates, security alerts, and changes to our Terms or this Policy.
12. **Support Responses** – Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.

We do not guarantee that AI-generated meme content is accurate, appropriate, or suitable for any purpose. Always review content before saving or sharing.

When you use Leon, your prompts and meme requests are sent to third-party AI providers for processing. This includes:

## 6. Data Retention

* **Account Data** – Retained for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period after deletion for legal and operational purposes (typically 90 days).
* **Meme Content & History** – Retained indefinitely while your account is active. Upon account deletion, meme data is deleted within 90 days.
* **Prompts & Generation Data** – May be retained indefinitely for service improvement, analytics, and model training.
* **Training Copies** – Anonymized excerpts used for model development may be retained for the life of the model.
* **Support Communications** – Retained as long as necessary to resolve issues and provide ongoing support.
* **Subscription & Billing Records** – Retained as required by law (typically 7 years for tax purposes).
* **Aggregated Analytics** – Retained indefinitely in anonymized form.

We may retain certain data longer if required by law or to protect our legal interests.

## 7. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your information:

* **Encryption** – Data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
* **Access Controls** – Least-privilege access to personal data; authentication required for all internal systems.
* **Secure Authentication** – Apple Sign-In with JWT token validation via Apple's JWKS.
* **Secure Storage** – Sensitive tokens stored in device SecureStore (iOS Keychain).
* **Monitoring** – Continuous monitoring for security threats and unauthorized access.
* **Third-Party Security** – We select vendors with strong security practices and require data processing agreements.

**No system is perfectly secure.** While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Avoid including highly sensitive personal information in meme prompts.
